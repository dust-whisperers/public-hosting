What to expect, start to finish.

Thank you for trusting us with your home. Here is how your visit works and what helps it go well.

1
Two days before
We send a reminder with your date and arrival window.
2
On the day
Your cleaner arrives inside the window you picked. You don't need to be home.
3
During the clean
They work through everything in the package you booked, room by room.
4
Afterwards
You get a note when the clean is done, and a chance to tell us how it went.
Before we arrive
Four things that help
  • Tell us how to get in. A door code, a lockbox or a neighbor. Add it to your booking so your cleaner has it.
  • Tell us about your pets. Your cleaner will know before they arrive. Our products are pet-safe.
  • Clear the surfaces you want cleaned. Clothes, toys and dishes put away means the time goes to cleaning.
  • Put away valuables and private papers. Your cleaner may photograph their work, never anything confidential.
What we bring
One cleaner, one standard
  • Your own cleaner. Matched on your first booking, and you keep them.
  • Vetted, insured and bonded. Background-checked and interviewed. Fewer than 1 in 4 applicants pass.
  • All the supplies. Non-toxic, plant-based products at no extra charge. Prefer yours? Leave them out and we'll use them.
  • Tips are optional. If you leave one, your cleaner keeps all of it.
